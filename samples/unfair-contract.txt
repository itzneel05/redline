DESIGN SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into between Acme Studio ("Client") and the Designer.

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Designer will provide design services as requested by Client from time to time.
Client may request unlimited revisions until Client is satisfied with the deliverables.

2. FEES
Client shall pay the fee of $2,000 net 90 days from the date of invoice.

3. PAYMENT
Payment is contingent upon Client's receipt of payment from its own customers. Client
may withhold payment at its sole discretion if the deliverables are not to Client's
satisfaction.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work shall be considered work for hire and Designer assigns all right, title, and
interest in all intellectual property to Client, including any pre-existing tools,
templates, and components. Client grants back a perpetual, irrevocable licence.

5. ADDITIONAL WORK
Designer will make reasonable changes to the deliverables at no additional cost.

6. RESTRICTIONS
Designer shall not, during the term and for two years after, engage in any business that
competes with Client (non-compete), and shall provide services to Client on an exclusive
basis.

7. LIABILITY
Designer shall indemnify and hold harmless Client against any and all claims, and shall
be liable for all damages without limitation of liability.

8. TERM
This agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms. Client may
terminate this agreement at any time for convenience.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware and the parties submit to
the exclusive jurisdiction of its courts.
