FREELANCE DESIGN AGREEMENT

1. SERVICES
Designer will deliver the logo package described in Exhibit A: two initial concepts and
two rounds of revisions.

2. FEES
Client will pay 500 USD. Client pays 50% up front and 50% on delivery.

3. PAYMENT
Client will pay each invoice within 14 days.

4. OWNERSHIP
On final payment, Designer assigns Client the rights to the delivered logo artwork.
Designer keeps the right to show the work in a portfolio, and keeps ownership of any
pre-existing tools and templates.

5. CHANGES
Additional work beyond the two included revision rounds will be quoted and agreed in
writing before it starts.

6. TERMINATION
Either party may end this agreement with 14 days written notice. Client will pay for all
work completed up to that date.

7. LIABILITY
Designer's total liability is limited to the fees paid under this agreement.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement is governed by the laws of India.
